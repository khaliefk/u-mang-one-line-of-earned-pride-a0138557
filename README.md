## What it does

`main.py` writes a single original line to `/output/entry.txt`: a quiet,
earned moment of pride, in at most 240 characters.

Tomas has practiced the same song for twenty years on a borrowed guitar.
Tonight, in his mother's kitchen with his sisters listening from the
doorway, he plays it through without stopping once. The long effort is
inside the line, the scene is seen happening, and the pride lands on
the last words.

## The entry

> For twenty years Tomas practiced the same song on a borrowed guitar; tonight, in his mother's kitchen with his sisters listening from the doorway, he played it through without stopping once.

One sentence, 190 characters, no trailing newline in the output file.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```

`main.py` checks its own output before writing: the line must be a
single line of 1 to 240 characters, and the file holds exactly that
line. No network, no dependencies.
